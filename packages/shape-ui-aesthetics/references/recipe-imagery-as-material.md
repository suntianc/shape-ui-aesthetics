# Recipe: Imagery as Material

This recipe is exemplar-derived vocabulary, not a default; adopt, adapt, or reject it in service of the established aesthetic thesis.

> **Evidence sites:** aesop.com, nothing.tech, apple.com/airpods-pro, teenage.engineering, daylightcomputer.com, ndc.co.jp, nakagawa-masashichi.jp, toraya-group.co.jp, supabase.com, amie.so, stripe.com, resend.com (all captured 2026-07). **Counter-examples:** frankchimero.com, paco.me, ia.net (captured 2026-07). All values and observations were taken from each site's captured profile.
>
> **Companion:** this recipe is the content-side half of the near-white single-accent recipe's two-ledger discipline — that recipe's Disciplines item 1 governs the layout ledger (chroma zeroed, accent by slot); this one governs how imagery in the content ledger carries the color and material budget. The two are meant to be used as a pair.

Scope note: this recipe covers imagery only (photography, illustration, 3D rendered objects, abstract graphics); audio was ruled out for insufficient evidence and is not covered at all.

## System proposition

Treat imagery as the **system's material and color supply layer**, not decorative patches: the aesthetic thesis first selects the image type and its world (color temperature, lighting, material, compositional language), and imagery then takes on what the layout deliberately declines to do — emotional color, warmth, whitespace, physical presence. Every image must be able to answer "what does it supply to the system."

## Applies when

- **A physical product or scene exists to shoot or render**, and physical credibility is the core of persuasion: hardware commerce (nothing.tech, teenage.engineering, apple.com/airpods-pro), craft and food heritage houses (toraya-group.co.jp, nakagawa-masashichi.jp), care brands (aesop.com), anti-screen hardware (daylightcomputer.com), a design firm's portfolio (ndc.co.jp).
- **The layout runs zero-chroma or single-accent discipline** (the dark monochrome, near-white single-accent, and paper serif families) and needs imagery to carry all emotional color and every seasonal or campaign variation — nothing.tech, toraya-group.co.jp, ndc.co.jp, and aesop.com all hold the UI's chromatic budget at zero; color lives only in the images.
- **An intangible technical product needs physical expression:** a developer API builds "premium black print" physicality from glowing 3D objects (resend.com); financial infrastructure uses a gradient ribbon as ambient light (stripe.com); a database tool uses gray line-art illustration to convey concepts without stealing color (supabase.com).
- **An AI-built site has no existing photography and needs generated images as the supply layer** — this recipe's core service scenario: generated images must be derived and accepted under the five-element discipline (Disciplines item 6); otherwise prefer the no-imagery route (Disciplines item 7).

## Does not apply — with live counter-examples

- **The content is the words; the work is the thought:** frankchimero.com's only "graphic" sitewide is the external-link arrow ↗ — two gray levels, one serif, and two-thirds whitespace are the entire statement (frankchimero.com, captured 2026-07); paco.me's home page carries zero images and zero color, with all the craft hidden in the token layer (paco.me, captured 2026-07). Forcing "mood imagery" onto such a site is a temperament mismatch: pure typography is a choice, not a deficiency.
- **Aesthetic identity is already carried independently by typography:** ia.net does place a product-evidence image in each section, but the site's identity comes from its 22px/1.65 body — "typography is the product"; the images serve only as evidence, never as material (ia.net, captured 2026-07). This is the legitimate text end of the spectrum (see Parameter bands, the image-to-text ratio row).
- **Persuasion runs on an evidence chain, not material atmosphere:** supabase.com's real screenshots, real code, and real tweets, and amie.so's real email screenshots with handwritten annotations, give images the identity of "evidence" — inserting information-free atmospheric material shots here dilutes the sense of evidence; material imagery and evidence imagery are two identities that must not be mixed.
- **A unified image world cannot be guaranteed:** prefer no imagery (see Known failure modes, mode 1 — careless imagery destroys a system faster than none).

## Perceptual grounding

- **Color, light, and material (relational color):** the supply discipline stands on the same perceptual basis as the near-white single-accent recipe — color salience is a relative quantity. Once layout chroma is zeroed, the amber-brown of aesop.com's photography, the pale-pink and pale-cyan seasonal backing papers of toraya-group.co.jp, and the product yellow of nothing.tech earn full attention with no UI cooperation; if the layout supplies color at the same time, the two ledgers cancel each other out.
- **Spatial composition (ownership of whitespace):** negative space inside the frame is an extension of layout whitespace — ndc.co.jp outsources its "white" to the sea and sky inside its photography, keeping only a 7% white rail as a marker; toraya-group.co.jp's high-whitespace studio shots let a one-image-one-line section still not read as empty. When selecting or generating an image, composition is typesetting.
- **Meaning, culture, and aesthetic thesis (image type as temperament claim):** photography conveys the material warmth and trust of the tangible (aesop.com's amber bottles, nakagawa-masashichi.jp's documentary life scenes); illustration conveys concept and approachability at controllable chroma (supabase.com's gray line art, teenage.engineering's comics); 3D rendered objects give an intangible product a body (resend.com); abstract gradients serve infrastructure products as ambient light (stripe.com). Type selection is downstream of the thesis, not of the asset library.
- **Coherence, critique, and evidence (enumerable means auditable):** nothing.tech's four lighting setups, teenage.engineering's two display grounds, and toraya-group.co.jp's single studio language all keep "which template does this image belong to" answerable; in AI-generation scenarios this clause upgrades from taste to a survival line — without an enumerable world, batch generation inevitably diverges.

## Disciplines

1. **Every image first answers "what does it supply":** emotional color, material warmth, in-frame whitespace, physical presence, or evidence — if it can answer none of these, delete it. An image is a system component, not a decorative patch.
2. **Color-supplying images enter the color ledger and coordinate with the layout's grayscale hierarchy.** When the layout runs zero chroma, the imagery's color temperature and dominant hue must be managed like tokens: aesop.com's gradient darkening guarantees the white-text hierarchy still holds over photography; nothing.tech's dot-matrix watermark auto-inverts over light and dark photography — the value relationship between image layer and layout layer is designed, not stumbled into. This item pairs with the near-white single-accent recipe's Disciplines item 1: that item guarantees the layout yields; this one guarantees the imagery can receive.
3. **One world only:** all imagery sitewide shares one color temperature, lighting, material, and compositional language; variation is permitted only inside **enumerated** lighting or backing templates (nothing.tech rotates four templates to keep a long page from going monotone; toraya-group.co.jp changes backing paper, never lighting). Looking at any single image, you should be able to say which template it belongs to.
4. **In-frame negative space is a typesetting resource:** when selecting and composing, treat whitespace inside the frame as layout whitespace (ndc.co.jp's sea and sky are the page's breathing room); an image meant to carry text must have its low-detail zone reserved at shooting or generation time, not patched afterward with an overlay.
5. **One primary route; secondary routes hold jurisdictions:** teenage.engineering's skeleton is always the cool-gray catalog, with comics, tickets, and collage admitted only into campaign banners; nothing.tech's dot-matrix watermark works only in the material layer, never the content layer. Outside its jurisdiction, everything returns to the primary route.
6. **The five-element discipline for AI-generated imagery** (this recipe's core service scenario; derived by transferring the art-direction unity of nothing.tech / toraya-group.co.jp / aesop.com into the AI scenario — marked as a derived clause):
   - Prompts must derive **five elements** from the aesthetic thesis: color temperature (warm/cool/neutral, with a reference color), lighting template (which of the enumerated templates in Parameter bands it belongs to), material keywords (paper / metal / fabric / skin …), composition and negative-space placement (reserved for text-over-image), and a chroma ceiling (full chroma for color-supplying images, gray-bound for supporting ones). Generic prompts of the "beautiful photo of X" kind are forbidden.
   - **All images in one batch share one set of five elements, changing only the subject** — the target is "shot by the same photographer on the same day."
   - **Every generated image must be accepted against the five elements, and regenerated on failure;** when acceptance fails and the regeneration budget is spent, degrade to no imagery (item 7) — never to "good enough."
7. **No imagery is a legitimate option:** when nothing physical exists to shoot, when the content is the words, or when a unified world cannot be guaranteed, pure typography is the higher choice (frankchimero.com and paco.me at zero images; ia.net's typography bearing the weight) — empty beats wrong.

## Known failure modes

1. **Careless AI images destroy a system faster than none** — each image with its own color temperature, lighting, and style; the noise of the image layer overturns the entire layout discipline, and the more restrained the layout (the dark monochrome / near-white / paper serif families), the more glaring the clash. Contrast frankchimero.com and paco.me: they prove zero imagery fully stands — "there has to be an image" is a false requirement.
2. **Generic prompts produce stock-photo feel:** a five-element-free "professional photo, high quality" generation has random color temperature, centered composition, and no negative space — a template site at a glance. Not one image across the evidence sites is "generically good-looking"; every one is downstream of a thesis (aesop.com's amber apothecary, nothing.tech's transparent industrial design).
3. **Two-headed color supply:** the layout keeps an accent while images erect a high-saturation dominant of their own — two ledgers fighting, and the near-white single-accent recipe's ledger discipline is breached from the image side. Positive contrast: daylightcomputer.com, whose single amber accent coexists with warm photographs of the same family.
4. **Illustration chroma runaway:** color-filled illustration steals the accent's scarcity; supabase.com can afford illustrated bento cards precisely because every illustration is gray line art.
5. **Image-text fence-sitting:** unwilling to let photography bear the weight at 95:5 like ndc.co.jp, unwilling to stand on pure typography like paco.me, so each section gets one mechanical mid-shot — both ends of the spectrum hold; the stanceless middle does not.
6. **Rendered-object hodgepodge:** a different 3D style per section (isometric illustration + glassmorphism + photoreal renders interleaved) — contrast resend.com's single glowing form reused sitewide.
7. **Single-site cloning:** lifting nothing.tech's dot-matrix watermark + frosted-glass cards + four lighting setups wholesale is cloning, not distillation. What transfers from this recipe is the logic — imagery supplies color and material under one world while the layout yields; that aesop.com's amber, toraya-group.co.jp's seasonal backing papers, and resend.com's 3D highlight parameters all differ is the proof.


## Usage rulings

- 2026-07-26, Tandoor trial, second human review round: when no real product screenshot exists, filling a feature card's mid-zone evidence area with a low-chroma abstract DOM schematic (instead of a fabricated UI screenshot) was approved in direction, but ruled insufficient in detail on its own. Ruling: schematics must ship as **schematic plus micro-caption** — each functional schematic carries explanatory micro-copy at the lowest type tier so the zone explains itself; captions stay inside the factual boundary (no invented dish names or data) and must not break the color ledger. Generalized rule: neither placeholders nor schematics may be mute graphics — a substitute visual without explanatory text hides a detail debt inside a style choice.
